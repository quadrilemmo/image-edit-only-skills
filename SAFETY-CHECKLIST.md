# Safety Checklist

Use this before every edit pass.

## Edit-only checklist
- [ ] I am editing existing pixels only
- [ ] I am not inventing new objects
- [ ] I am not creating a new background from scratch
- [ ] I am not changing the subject identity
- [ ] I am not altering branding or labels unless requested
- [ ] I am preserving shadows and perspective where possible
- [ ] I am keeping the result believable and natural

## Crop checklist
- [ ] Subject remains intact
- [ ] Crop is consistent with the set standard
- [ ] No important detail is cut off
- [ ] Framing remains realistic

## Background checklist
- [ ] Background is clean and even
- [ ] Subject edges remain natural
- [ ] No pasted-on effect is introduced
- [ ] Background remains consistent across the set

## Color and tone checklist
- [ ] White balance is consistent with target
- [ ] Exposure is visually aligned across images
- [ ] Contrast is balanced and realistic
- [ ] Saturation is not exaggerated

## Sharpness checklist
- [ ] Sharpness enhancement is subtle
- [ ] No halos or artifacts are introduced
- [ ] Focus remains natural and believable

## Batch checklist
- [ ] All images obey the same crop standard
- [ ] Set is visually unified
- [ ] Background tone is consistent
- [ ] Quality and consistency are verified
- [ ] Manifest/report is generated

## Final fail-fast rule

If the edit would require generating new content, inventing missing detail, or replacing the source subject, stop and reject the request.
